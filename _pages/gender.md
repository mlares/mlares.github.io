This is the page for the project "gender in Argentina"

Presentation in the ["Congreso de Ciencia y Género"](https://congresodecienciaygenero.cba.gov.ar):


[Presentation (PDF, spanish)]({% link /documents/CCyG2021.pdf %})


Conclusions:

- In the astronomical community in Argentina there is no evidence for 
a systematic gender based discrimination.
- The data used to arrive at this conclusion include: Academic data
  (UNC), workplace data and career categories (CONICET) and
  publications (ADS).
