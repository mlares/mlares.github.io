pipeline_pagos.get_services(**options)[source]¶

This function builds the services dictionary, which is a simple dict of names-to-implementation used by bonobo for runtime injection. It will be used on top of the defaults provided by bonobo (fs, http, …). You can override those defaults, or just let the framework define them. You can also define your own services and naming is up to you.

Returns:

dict
