## A path through science and data

I was born in Alta Gracia and earned my PhD in Astronomy at Universidad Nacional de Córdoba. As a researcher at CONICET, I worked with large astronomical datasets, statistical models, and scientific software in international collaborations.

That work led into applied data science: leading an eight-person team at IThreex Global, then developing recommendation models and guiding production experiments at Mercado Libre. Across these settings, I’ve worked on turning complex questions into models, software, and evidence people can use.

## Learning together

I’ve taught for twenty years at Universidad Nacional de Córdoba, where I head the Data Science course in Applied Mathematics. Teaching, mentoring researchers, and building teams are central parts of my work. The [interactive lab]({{ '/lab/' | relative_url }}) brings some of those ideas into experiments you can try yourself.
